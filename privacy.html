Privacy PolicyTerms of Use

This policy describes how QuietRead: Read & Listen handles information when you create speech, read documents, find books, and use ad-removal purchases.

1. Documents and speech on your device

Text, imported documents, drafts, generated audio, voice preferences, and reading position are stored on your device. After the required Supertonic 3 package is downloaded, verified, and installed, speech is generated on the device. QuietRead does not send document content, titles, local paths, phonemes, or content hashes to advertising services or analytics.

If you choose to export or share a file, you select its destination or receiving application. Operating-system backups depend on your device settings.

2. Features that use the network

Network connections download the required voice package, search online book catalogs, download books you select, and fetch PDF links you provide. Search terms and request network information are sent to the relevant book provider. Text used to generate speech is not included in book-search requests.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

The free version uses Google AdMob and UMP. These SDKs may process IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising interactions, and diagnostic data. Consent choices depend on your region. You can change choices in Settings when advertising privacy options are available. See Google's privacy policy.

The app does not request or display ads during setup, onboarding, a paywall, or active audio playback. App-initiated ad requests are at least five minutes apart. An active ad-removal purchase suppresses ads.

4. Purchases

Apple processes payments. QuietRead receives product information and verified transactions to determine ad-removal access and restore purchases; it does not receive your payment card number. The weekly subscription renews until cancelled in the App Store. Lifetime access is a one-time purchase. Displayed prices come from your App Store region.

RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app user identifier, product and transaction information, and Apple receipts to reconcile purchases and measure revenue. For ads, the app reports the ad unit identifier, a random per-ad identifier, load/display/tap/failure events, and revenue amount, currency, and precision supplied by Google. Network requests may include an IP address and technical SDK information. QuietRead does not send email addresses, the IDFA advertising identifier, or document content to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

5. Optional analytics

Firebase Analytics is off by default. If you enable “Share usage statistics” during onboarding or in Settings, the app measures onboarding steps, the screen being used, audio generation outcomes, playback actions, import file types, book catalog providers, and purchase/restore actions. Custom events use predefined categories only; they contain no search queries, book titles, content, local paths, document IDs, or raw error messages.

The SDK may collect session information, app/OS versions, device model, approximate region inferred from network requests, in-app purchases, and a random app-installation identifier. QuietRead disables Firebase IDFA/IDFV collection and ad-personalization signals. TestFlight and App Store data use separate projects. See Firebase data collection information.

You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings. The app stops collection, clears local analytics data, and resets the analytics identifier. Actions taken while analytics is off are not replayed later. This does not delete data already sent to Google or change advertising choices or purchases.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data is kept so you can resume work and listen again. You can delete documents or audio with the app's delete controls. Uninstalling removes the app's local storage; files you exported, shared, or backed up may remain where you saved them.

Providers such as Apple, Google, and RevenueCat handle and retain their service data under their own policies. Manage subscriptions in the App Store and advertising preferences through the app's available privacy options. For questions or deletion requests concerning purchase or analytics data, use the support link below. For requests involving personal information, ask for a private contact method before sharing that information; deleting data does not cancel an App Store subscription.

7. Changes and contact

We will update this page when our data practices change. The latest update date appears above.
